    Ceylon Tea's new flavors are waiting for you as you "Discover the World in Every Sip!"


    🌿☕☀️🌎Our new line of flavoured Ceylon teas! We are excited to present a tantalising selection of flavours that will elevate your tea-drinking experience. Each blend, made with the best Ceylon tea leaves, is a taste-delighting fusion of innovation and tradition meant to take you on a trip across the world with every sip.🌎☀️☕🌿


    Early gray tea


    You are met with a harmonious blend of sharp, powerful black tea and the bright, citrusy notes of bergamot when you first sip an Earl Grey tea. The rich flavor profile successfully balances the tea's natural astringency with the zingy citrus undertone. It has a strong, energizing flavor with a tinge of sweetness.

    cinnamon flavoured tea


    You are greeted by a harmonious fusion of the natural qualities of the tea base and the smoky, sweet notes of cinnamon when you sip cinnamon-flavored tea. The flavor profile evokes the relaxing spice, and it frequently provides a warming, calming effect. Depending on the tea blend and the preparation method, the cinnamon flavor can range in intensity from a faint undertone to a more noticeable spiciness.

    jasmin flavoured tea


    The flavors of Ceylon Jasmine Flavored Tea come together beautifully when it is brewed. A faint malty undertone and a quick, slightly astringent flavor are provided by the Ceylon tea base. The jasmine infusion, though, is the real star. Every sip is infused with the floral richness of the jasmine petals, making for a superbly harmonious and aromatic tea experience. It strikes the ideal balance between the two, being neither too flowery nor overly tannic.

    lemon flavoured tea


    When brewed, Ceylon Lemon Flavored Tea offers a flavorful medley. A powerful and brisk flavor with a light astringency and a whiff of natural sweetness are provided by the Ceylon tea base. The lemon infusion commands attention and provides a burst of vibrant brightness that counteracts the profundity of the tea. It has an energizing and tangy flavor profile because to the tantalizing marriage of robust black tea and citrus lemon.

    mango flavoured tea


    Ceylon Mango Flavored Tea creates a flavorful union that works well together. A powerful and robust flavor with a modest astringency and natural sweetness is provided by the Ceylon tea base. The mango infusion, the star of the show, adds a blast of tropical sweetness and gives the tea a rich and juicy quality. The profundity of the tea is balanced out by the vibrant and delicious notes of the mango in this lovely combo.

    passion flavoured tea


    A delightful blend of flavors is provided by Ceylon Passion Fruit Flavored Tea. A strong and brisk character with a light astringency and a whiff of natural sweetness are provided by the Ceylon tea base. The focus is on the passion fruit infusion, which provides a rush of tropical sweetness and tanginess that counteracts the complexity of the tea. The deep, malty notes of Ceylon tea are well paired with the exotic, vibrant flavors of passion fruit in this wonderful brew.

    stawberry flavoured tea


    Ceylon Strawberry Flavored Tea gives a delicious mix of flavors when it is brewed. A robust and brisk character with a light astringency and a whiff of natural sweetness are provided by the Ceylon tea base. The main attraction is the strawberry infusion, which adds a burst of sweet and luscious strawberry flavor and deepens the tea's flavor. The sweet and delicious aroma of strawberries is well paired with the deep, malty notes of Ceylon tea.

    vanilla flavoured tea


    Ceylon Vanilla Flavored Tea gives a flavorful fusion that works well together. A powerful and brisk flavor with a light astringency and natural sweetness are provided by the Ceylon tea base. The sweetness, creaminess, and hint of formality added by the vanilla infusion, which takes centre stage, go well with the tea's complexity. The strength of Ceylon tea with the calming and fragrant flavour of vanilla make for a lovely blend.
