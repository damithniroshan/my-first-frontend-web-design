    kandy black tea

    ★ ★ ★ ★ ★

    The well-balanced and strong flavor of Kandy black tea defines its flavor profile. It has a medium to full-bodied infusion and a quick, vivacious personality. Expect a well-balanced combination of maltiness, delicate citrus undertones, and a mild astringency. Black tea from Kandy is adaptable and may be consumed either plain or with milk and sugar, meeting a range of preferences.

    CEYLON GOLDREN TIP TEA

    ★ ★ ★ ★ ★

    Young tea leaves and buds are often used to make Golden Tip tea. These buds feature fine hairs and a higher level of natural sugars, which give them a golden or bronze look. To provide a distinct flavor character, the leaves are frequently more heavily oxidized than those of other black teas.


    Price strating from : $24.00 (250g)


    CEYLON silver TIP TEA

    ★ ★ ★ ★ ★

    Only unopened tea plant buds are used to make Silver Tip Tea. These buds have a distinctive appearance due to the little, silvery hairs that cover them. To retain their original flavor and look, the buds are meticulously picked and dried.


    Price strating from : $24.00 (250g)


    CEYLON white TEA

    ★ ★ ★ ★ ★

    Young leaves and unopened buds of the tea plant (Camellia sinensis) are used to make Ceylon White Tea. It only goes through little processing, including drying and withering. To ensure that only the newest and most delicate pieces are used, the tea leaves are meticulously hand-picked.

    flavoured tea

    ★ ★ ★ ★ ★

    A delicious and fragrant type of tea from Sri Lanka, formerly known as Ceylon, is Ceylon-flavored tea. High-quality Ceylon black tea leaves are used to make this tea because they are known for their astringency and vibrant, coppery liquid. The addition of alluring, natural flavours that improve the tea's flavour and aroma is what distinguishes Ceylon flavoured tea from other varieties.Ceylon flavoured tea offers a wide variety of flavours since it is flavoured with a wide variety of ingredients, including fruits, flowers, spices, and essential oils. Ceylon black tea is sometimes combined with tropical fruits like mango, passion fruit, or pineapple to give it a sweet and fruity flavour. Earl Grey, which combines Ceylon tea with bergamot oil for a citrusy note, is another well-known variety.
